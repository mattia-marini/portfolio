<!-- compact -->A large collection of `LaTeX` notes for high school tutoring and bachelor students, covering calculus, linear algebra, physics, computer science, and more.<!-- /compact -->

Some of the most notable ones include:

- [ripetizioni](https://github.com/latex-workspace/ripetizioni) — a collection of custom-made notes for students on various topics, such as [ICT](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/latex-workspace/ripetizioni/refs/heads/main/Danny/main.pdf) and [math & physics](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/latex-workspace/ripetizioni/refs/heads/main/Emma/main.pdf)
- [probabilità e statistica](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/latex-workspace/probabilita-statistica/refs/heads/main/main.pdf) — university notes for probability and statistics
