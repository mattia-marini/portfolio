<!-- compact -->
A performance-focused hypergraph library, written in `Rust` and designed to be exposed through `Python` bindings.
<!-- /compact -->

Key highlights include:

- **State-of-the-art** exact motif counting on weighted and unweighted hypergraphs
- Extremely type-safe implementations without detriment to performance
- `SIMD`-accelerated graphlet fingerprint heuristics

This project aims to be a `Rust` rewrite of the [HypergraphX](https://github.com/HGX-Team/hypergraphx) Python library.
