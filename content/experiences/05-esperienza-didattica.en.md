## Overview

Since 2022 I have been tutoring high school students in one-on-one sessions covering mathematics, physics, and computer science. This experience has allowed me to develop not only communication and teaching skills, but also a deeper reflection on how computer science can serve as an **effective vehicle for learning** other disciplines.

## Main activities

- One-on-one lessons in mathematics and physics for high school students
- Explaining *programming* and *algorithms* concepts to students at all levels, from beginners to advanced
- Adapting teaching methods to each student's needs and skill level

## Results

- Visible improvement in the academic performance of students I worked with
- Development of communication skills and the ability to adapt to individual needs
- Deeper understanding of computer science as a *teaching tool* rather than just a technical discipline
