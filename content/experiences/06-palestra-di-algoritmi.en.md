## Overview

In 2025 I delivered several training sessions, organized in collaboration with the **University of Trento**, for students at **Liceo Bertrand Russell** preparing for the **Informatics Olympiad**. This activity was particularly meaningful because, more than any other experience, it tested my ability to simplify extremely complex concepts and make them accessible to people with less experience than myself. This pushed me to experiment with different teaching approaches and tools, aiming to reduce barriers to understanding as much as possible. The experience brought me closer to *teaching* — understood as the search for the most effective learning path with the least amount of friction.

## Main activities

* Designing lab sessions on *advanced algorithms* and *data structures*
* Preparing exercises and training problems inspired by past **OII** editions
* One-on-one support for students exploring specific topics in depth

## Results

* Development of *problem solving* and algorithmic reasoning skills in participating students
* Creation of reusable teaching materials for future training activities, available [here](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/latex-workspace/palestra-algoritmi/refs/heads/main/main.pdf)
* Growth in teaching and communication skills for complex algorithmic concepts
