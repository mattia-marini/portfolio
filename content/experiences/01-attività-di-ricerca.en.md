## Overview

Starting in September 2025, I began a research activity in the field of **hypergraph** algorithms. The work is part of my bachelor's thesis and focuses on higher-order *motif* analysis in large weighted and unweighted hypergraphs — a problem with applications in bioinformatics, social network analysis, and neuroscience.

## Main activities

- Designing efficient combinatorial algorithms for *motif* counting and analysis on hypergraphs
- Introducing `Rust` into the already mature [HypergraphX](https://github.com/HGX-Team/hypergraphx) hypergraph library
- Experimental validation on real-world datasets, comparing performance against the existing state of the art
- Writing a scientific paper documenting the results

## Results

- Establishing a new **state of the art** for efficiency and scalability in weighted hypergraph *motif* analysis
- Performant *open-source* implementation of the developed algorithms
- Contribution to the scientific community with potential publications in journals in the field
