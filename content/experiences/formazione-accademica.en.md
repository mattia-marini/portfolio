## Overview

Since 2022 I have been a Computer Science student at the **University of Trento**, where I completed all curriculum exams with a weighted average of **29.05/30**. My academic journey allowed me to explore both the theoretical and practical sides of computer science, with a particular interest in *algorithms*, *data structures*, and *computational complexity*.

## Path

While university was essential for building a solid foundation across the main areas of computer science, extracurricular activities and projects developed in parallel played an equally important formative role.

During my university years I independently developed several projects, ranging:

* from **extensions and plugins aimed at other developers**;
* to **digital musical pedals**, later used in live performances.

These experiences led me to develop two main interests:

* **Software engineering**

  * Interest in aspects that go beyond just writing code.
  * Understanding of the **collaborative and complex** nature of software development.
  * Interest in managing large codebases and the role of **design in reducing cognitive complexity**.

* **Algorithms and elegant solutions**

  * Aptitude for problems that admit **elegant and efficient** solutions.
  * Interest in the intersection of **algorithmic elegance, mathematical rigor, and concrete needs**.
  * Pursuit of solutions where design quality directly contributes to system performance.

This path led me to specialize in **low-level programming**, often combined with the development of **high-performance algorithms and systems**.

## Results

- Exams completed with a weighted average of **29.05/30**
- Creation of a `Rust` fork of the well-known [HypergraphX](https://github.com/HGX-Team/hypergraphx) library, with significant performance improvements
- Solid theoretical and practical preparation for pursuing a career in academia or education
